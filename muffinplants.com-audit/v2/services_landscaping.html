Landscaping Services

Gardens, balconies and lawns that suit your space and your life.

We design and plant gardens, balconies and lawns in Karachi. The plants are picked for your light, your space and the time you have.

Chat on WhatsAppCall now

What's included

  • A site visit to look at light, space and soil
  • A planting plan for your garden, balcony or lawn
  • Plants chosen to cope with Karachi heat and sun
  • Soil preparation and planting
  • Beds, pots and lawn set up as agreed
  • A simple care sheet for what comes next

How it works

  1. 1

    Message us

    Tell us about your space and what you have in mind. Photos help.

  2. 2

    We visit and quote

    We look at the space in person, talk it through with you and send a quote.

  3. 3

    We plan and plant

    Once you are happy with the plan, we prepare the soil and put the plants in.

  4. 4

    Handover

    We show you how it all works and leave a simple care sheet.

Good for

  • New homes with bare outdoor space
  • Balconies and rooftops that need some life
  • Lawns that have given up
  • Anyone who wants a garden without the guesswork

Questions

Can you work with a small balcony?

Yes. Small spaces are some of our favourite jobs. A few well-chosen plants can change how a balcony feels.

Can I choose the plants myself?

Of course. Tell us what you like and we will say what will do well in your light and how much care each one needs.

How much does landscaping cost?

Quote on request. Every space is different, so we give you a quote after we have seen it.

How long will the work take?

Timeline confirmed after a site visit. It depends on the size of the space and what we are planting.

Ready to talk about landscaping?

Send us a message. We reply on WhatsApp and take it from there.
