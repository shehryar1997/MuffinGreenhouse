Plant Health Guarantee

We stand behind every plant we ship. If your plant arrives dead or severely damaged, we have got you covered. Just share clear photos within 2 hours of receiving your package, then return the plant to us and we'll make it right.

  • 2-hour window from the moment you receive your package
  • Clear photos of the plant and its packaging, sent on WhatsApp with your order number
  • Return the plant to us, then choose a replacement or store credit
WhatsApp for Claims

The Root Check Rule

If roots are white/tan and firm (healthy) but leaves decline after arrival, that is acclimation stress, not transit damage. We will not replace it, but we will guide you through recovery with a free plant doctor consultation included.

We Cover

  • • Broken/cracked stems from transit
  • • Root rot (black/brown mushy roots)
  • • Major pest infestation present at arrival
  • • Wrong plant shipped
  • • Severe leaf damage (>50% of foliage)
  • • Plant arrived dead or dying

We Do Not Cover

  • • Minor leaf damage (they recover)
  • • 1-2 yellow leaves on older plants
  • • Soil spillage during transit
  • • Decline after acclimation period
  • • Plants kept in unsuitable conditions
  • • Claims made more than 2 hours after you received the package
  • • Plants that aren't returned to us
